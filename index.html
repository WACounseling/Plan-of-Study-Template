2Prior Credit & Placement

From Woodward Middle School

Transfer credit (coursework from another school in grades 9–12, including approved summer forward credit)

If the course matches a Woodward course, choose it so prerequisites are satisfied (for example Biology or Algebra 2). Otherwise pick a subject area and enter credits. Transfer work appears as TR- on the transcript and is not included in WA GPAs.

3Four-Year Plan

Year-long courses are chosen in the Fall / Full Year dropdown and automatically fill both semesters. For semester courses, choose a Fall course and a Spring course. Items marked ⚑ approval are available but need department chair / instructor approval. Courses whose prerequisites are not yet met appear greyed out at the end of each list with the reason.

★Graduation Distinctions (optional)

Woodward students can earn a “Distinction in Global Studies, Service Learning, Sustainability or Media” read at graduation. Tick any you are interested in: its required courses are listed beneath it and turn bold with a ✓ as they appear in the four-year plan, and a pop-up appears when all of a distinction’s courses are planned. The other requirements (applications, hours, capstone projects, portfolios, averages) are a checklist in the Google Doc.

5Review & Submit

Submitting creates a Google Doc in your Google Drive (you are the owner and can edit it) and shares it view-only with the student. Page 1 is the four-year plan; page 2 is the graduation, scholarship and NCAA reference. You must be signed in with Google (Step 1).

Changed courses with the dropdowns in a Google Doc you created? This recalculates its graduation and distinction trackers in place.

Drafts are saved privately in your own Google Drive, in a folder named “WA Course Planner Drafts.” They are never shared with students and nothing is saved to this computer’s Downloads folder.

Planning tool only — the Upper School Scheduling & Curriculum Guide and the Academic Dean’s office govern. Student information is sent only to Google (your Drive) when a plan is submitted — it does not pass through any other server. Drafts stay in this browser tab.
